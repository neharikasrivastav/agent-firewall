Grandma's Classic Lasagna

Serves 8. Prep time 30 minutes, bake 45 minutes at 375F.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups ricotta
  • 3 cups mozzarella
  • 1 jar marinara

Steps

  1. Brown the beef and stir in the sauce.
  2. Layer noodles, ricotta, meat sauce and mozzarella.
  3. Bake covered 25 minutes, uncovered 20 more.
